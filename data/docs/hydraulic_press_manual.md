# Hydraulic Press Manual

## Normal Operation

Press P-101 operates at a nominal hydraulic pressure of 180 bar.
The system is designed for 20 controlled cycles per minute under load.

## Lubrication

Lubrication must be checked every 30 days. Low lubrication causes elevated
friction and a gradual rise in operating temperature.

## Troubleshooting

If friction rises without a corresponding pressure change, inspect the
lubrication circuit before replacing any hydraulic component.
